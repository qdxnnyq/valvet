# Changelog

## 1.9.21

Live board uses the history bubbles, with current rank, peak, and parties.

## 1.9.20

The live board is the 1.8.23 one again.

## 1.9.19

A live game leaves the waiting screen and shows the lobby.

## 1.9.18

A found match leaves the waiting screen and opens the live lobby.

## 1.9.17

The live lobby stays on screen when a match is found.

## 1.9.0

Play the round draws the kill lines again, with a glow on yours.
History stats are one short strip. Empty numbers stay off.
Bundles use the same cards as the store. Heat on the map is lighter.
Your share id stays hidden until you show it.
Each round video is its own file. Older clips are gathered into Videos\Valvet.

## 1.8.44

Parties are a colored line. Shots split into head, body, and legs.
The last act stays in its own folder. A penalty shows the minutes left.
The update check keeps the pink bar up if GitHub is slow to answer.

## 1.8.43

History filters use the dark menu. A name in the search finds that player's games.
Each map shows attack and defense under the minimap. Two players can sit side by side.
A bundle says how many pieces you own. The other team's agents copy as one line.
Drop an mp3 on a sound row. Clutches split into 1v1, 1v2, and 1v3.
Backup is one file: settings, watch list, wishes, crosshairs, and sounds.
Parties, names, and the live board stay on when stat lookups are paused.
The updater still reads the public version file.

## 1.8.42

History shows Win or Loss, and the cat, instead of an empty mark.
A clutch is only a round you were last alive. A lost one says Died.
The daily line is gone. Parties stay on during a match even if lookups are paused.
The wish button sits under the price.
